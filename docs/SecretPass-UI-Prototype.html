Terms of Service

The short version

SecretPass stores your passwords in a vault only you can open. Everything is scrambled on your device before it reaches us, using your master password as the key. We can't read your passwords — and neither can anyone who breaks into our servers.

How your vault stays private

Your master password never leaves your device. What we store is meaningless without it. Nobody at SecretPass can look inside your vault — not support, not engineers, not anyone.

Your master password and recovery key

We don't have a copy of your master password and can't reset it. That's why we give you a recovery key at setup — a one-time backup. Save it somewhere safe and offline.

If you lose both, your vault can't be recovered. Not by you, not by us. Any backdoor we built for that would be a backdoor someone else could use.

Your subscription

SecretPass is $5 a month or $50 a year, and every feature is included at both prices. You subscribe on our website and sign in anywhere. Cancel any time — you keep full access until the period you've paid for ends.

Not for you? Cancel within 30 days and we'll refund you in full, automatically. The refund window is how you try SecretPass without risk.

If your subscription ends, you don't lose your passwords. Your vault stays readable on the device you're already using, and you can export it at any time. Syncing and the browser extension stop until you resubscribe.

What we collect

The minimum we need: your email, your subscription status, and basic sync information. Not your vault contents — we can't read those.

Ending things

Delete your account any time from settings, and we delete your vault data from our servers.
